Meet the Author

Terry McCall-El

Terry McCall-El is an independent author, publisher, storyteller, poet and creative entrepreneur. Through Black Tongue Publishing, he creates books and media that blend personal experience, imagination, culture, education and empowerment.

His catalog includes urban literature, poetry, children's books and forward-thinking work exploring artificial intelligence and modern technology.
