Meet the Author

Terry McCall-El

Terry McCall-El is an independent author, publisher, poet, songwriter, storyteller and creative entrepreneur.

Through Black Tongue Publishing, he develops books and media that combine culture, personal experience, imagination, education, technology and empowerment.

His catalog includes urban literature, poetry, children's stories and forward-thinking work exploring artificial intelligence.
